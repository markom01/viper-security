This policy explains what personal data VIPER Security collects through this website, how we use it, and the rights you have over it. We are committed to protecting your privacy in line with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Spanish and European law.

## Who we are

VIPER Security provides luxury chauffeur and executive transport services across Spain and Italy — airport transfers, corporate travel, VIP and villa transfers, and nightlife transport. You can reach us through the phone numbers and booking form on this site.

For any question about this policy or how we handle your data, contact us using the contact details on this site or the booking form on the home page.

## What we collect

When you use the booking form on this site, we collect the details you provide: your name, phone number, travel location, service, destination, and date. If you contact us by phone or WhatsApp, we may hold the details of that conversation.

## How we use your data

We use the information you provide for the following purposes:

- To respond to your booking request and arrange your chauffeur service.
- To confirm your transfer details and keep you informed.
- To contact you by phone or WhatsApp about your booking.
- To meet our legal and accounting obligations where a ride results from your booking.

Submitting the booking form opens a WhatsApp message to our chauffeur using the details you enter. The message is delivered directly through WhatsApp and is not processed further by this website.

## Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent when you submit a booking, and on the basis of performance of a contract when you ride with us. Where we have a legal obligation to retain records, we process data to comply with that obligation.

## Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We only share information where it is necessary to provide the service you requested, or where the law requires it.

## Data retention

We keep booking details only as long as needed to complete your ride and to meet any legal or accounting requirements.

## Your rights

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to ask for a copy of it. You may withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details on this site. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

## Cookies and analytics

This site does not set marketing cookies. It may use technical cookies or similar storage required for the site to function, and limited analytics may be used to understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## Security

We treat your data with care and apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect it against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and where relevant we will bring them to your attention.
